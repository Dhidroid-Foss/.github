# 🌱 Welcome to Dhidroid Community's GitHub!

**Dhidroid Community** is an open developer community for people who love to **build, learn, share, and contribute**.

We bring together developers, students, makers, AI enthusiasts, and open-source contributors to build useful technology together.

## What we do

- 💻 Build real-world software
- 🤖 Explore AI & emerging technologies
- 🌐 Create open-source projects
- 📚 Share knowledge and learning resources
- 🤝 Collaborate and mentor
- 🚀 Turn ideas into products

## Open Source is in our DNA

We believe technology grows when people build in the open.

Our projects are designed to be **community-driven, collaborative, and accessible to everyone** — from first-time contributors to experienced developers.

## Explore Dhidroid

🛠️ **Projects** — Community-built software and experiments

🌱 **Open Source** — Contributions, collaboration, and community projects
